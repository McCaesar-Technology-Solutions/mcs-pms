# Guest guide — MOJO Apartments

Welcome. You use a **simple mobile portal** — no password. Front desk gives you a **link or QR code** when you check in.

Tap **Help** (bottom-right) if you get stuck.

---

## 1. Getting into the portal

### How access works

1. Staff checks you in (reservation or walk-in).
2. You receive a **link** or **QR code**.
3. Personal link looks like: `{website}/guest/enter?token=...`
4. Lobby QR opens `{website}/guest/join/{property}` — enter your **room** and **last name** (or PIN if asked).
5. Opens **Guest portal** (`/guest`).

### House rules

If the property requires it, you must **read and accept house rules** before using the portal.

### No account

- No email/password to remember.
- The link is tied to **your stay**.

### When access stops

| Event | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Checkout day ends (midnight) | Link expires |
| Staff checks you out | Access stops |
| Staff revokes link | Link stops working |

**Link expired?** Ask front desk for a new one.

---

## 2. Five tabs

Use the **bottom tab bar**:

| Tab | What it’s for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Home** | Wi‑Fi, essentials, quick actions, call property |
| **Messages** | Chat with the front desk (general questions) |
| **My stay** | Dates, requests, invoices, Pay now |
| **Issues** | Report and track maintenance problems |
| **Account** | Phone, photo, Do Not Disturb, feedback, rules, guide |

**Badges** on tabs show pending requests, open issues, or new messages.

Staff may send a deep link that opens a specific tab (e.g. `/guest?tab=messages`).

### Two ways to message staff

| Use | For |
|-----|-----|
| **Messages** tab | Towels, checkout time, Wi‑Fi, general questions |
| **Issues** → open issue → **Message staff** | Updates about a specific repair |

---

## 3. Home

- Property name and welcome message.
- Your **room number** (and photo when available).
- **Quick actions** — request housekeeping, report an issue, message the team.
- **Essentials** — Wi‑Fi name/password, parking, emergency phone, checkout time.
- **Contact property** — call managers / reception.

---

## 4. Messages

- Chat with front desk about your stay.
- You can edit your own recent messages for a short time.
- Badges show when staff reply.
- Do **not** use this tab for a broken AC — use **Issues**.

---

## 5. My stay

### Stay info

- Check-in / check-out dates and timeline.
- Room assignment.

### Requests

Submit (when the property allows):

| Request | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Housekeeping** | Ask for cleaning / towels |
| **Late checkout** | Need more time on departure day |
| **Stay extension** | Add more nights |

Staff see requests on their dashboard and will confirm. Settling any balance still happens at the desk (or via Pay now when available).

### Billing

- View invoices for your stay (room, discounts, extras, Ghana tax when included).
- **Pay now** — if online payments are enabled and your invoice supports it (email may be required on your profile).
- Download PDF or **WhatsApp** a receipt when offered.
- Otherwise pay at front desk (cash, MoMo, card).

If the invoice includes Ghana tax, the Bill-to Tax ID shown is the hotel’s GRA tax ID.

---

## 6. Issues (maintenance)

### Report a problem

1. Choose a **category** (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, furniture, cleaning, noise, other).
2. Describe the issue (at least 10 characters).
3. Set **Normal** or **Urgent** priority.
4. Optionally attach a **photo**.
5. Submit — you get a **reference number**.

### Track progress

Watch status on the same tab. Use **Message staff** on an open issue for repair updates.

### Approve completion

When work is finished, you may be asked to **approve completion** so staff can close the issue. If something is still wrong, message staff instead of approving.

For urgent safety issues (flooding, no power), mark **Urgent**.

---

## 7. Account

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|----------------|
| **Phone number** | Ghana format e.g. `+233 24 123 4567` — helps staff reach you |
| **Profile photo** | Optional; shown in messages |
| **Do Not Disturb** | Staff see a flag — they should call before entering |
| **Feedback / rating** | Rate your stay (often after checkout) |
| **Property rules** | House rules again |
| **Local guide** | Nearby tips from the property |
| **Privacy / Terms** | Legal links |

---

## 8. Door access (if your property uses it)

Some properties sync door access when you check in.

- Your portal PIN may also work as a door PIN.
- Staff can assign a physical card.
- If the door won’t open, ask front desk — they can unlock remotely or retry sync.
- Access is removed when you check out.

---

## 9. Tips

- Tap a tab badge to see what needs attention.
- Keep your phone number current so technicians can reach you for repairs.
- Checkout still needs any balance settled — Pay now or front desk.
- If your link stops working, ask reception for a new one or use the lobby QR with your room and last name.
